05FAQ

Questions before you pay.

I don’t have TON. Can I still pay?

You’ll need a small amount of TON in a wallet. Telegram has a built-in wallet where you can get it; the Mini App walks you through it.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Everything starts in the Telegram Mini App. Native apps are optional if you want the VPN on more devices.

How many devices can I use?

As many as you like. Every plan includes unlimited devices.

What’s the difference between plans?

Only the length. Every plan has the same protocols, locations and devices.

Where do I get help?

Write to the support bot in Telegram. It’s linked from the Mini App menu.
